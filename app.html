❗ Activate Full Access Mode?
❗
Warning: Brief will control your entire real computer without asking before steps. In Full Access mode, the AI moves your real mouse, types with your real keyboard, edits and moves real files, and runs real terminal commands immediately.
  • Permanent deletion (files deleted permanently, Shift+Delete, or destructive terminal commands) will still always require confirmation.
  • You can halt the AI instantly at any millisecond by clicking ■ STOP or pressing your global hotkey (Ctrl+Alt+Q).
  • Full Access resets back to Ask for permission whenever you restart Brief.

Brief v2.0.0 — Windows, Linux X11 & Android (No Root), New Cursors, OpenRouter Look & Extra Thinking

✨ What’s New in v2.0.0
  • New Cursors (Zero Particles) & OpenRouter Look: Updated Gemini, Claude, and OpenRouter desktop cursors with crisp transparent edges (zero particles), plus OpenRouter's new bold white OR logo and obsidian-indigo theme.
  • Multi-Platform Brief (Windows · Linux X11 · Android Zero Root): Now downloadable for Windows, Linux X11 (.deb / .AppImage / .tar.gz), and Android 8+ / Samsung phones (Brief-Android.apk with floating provider cursors & Accessibility control).
  • Native Provider Themes & Fonts: Gemini (Google Sans + deep blue radial glow), OpenAI (pitch black + OpenAI Sans), Claude (warm charcoal + orange Send key & starburst), and OpenRouter (obsidian-indigo).
  • Extra Thinking & Usage Limit Ring: Choose Low, Medium, High, or Extra (deep parallel research + polish) alongside per-company Usage Limit tracking.
🔧 What Has Been Fixed
  • All 2026 Models Verified & Working: Same-tier runtime endpoint resolution ensures Gemini 4 (Hydrogen, Neon, Argon), Gemini 3.8 Flash, Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite, GPT-6 (Luna, 6.1 Terra, Sol, 6.2 Astra), and Claude 5.5 / Fable 5.2 work reliably without 404s or false "model does not exist" replies.
  • Fixed Slow Chat & Mid-Reply Cutoffs: Fixed the streaming timer bug that cut off replies mid-sentence, enabled IPv4-first networking, removed forced 2.6s web-search waits on simple chats, and sent explicit MINIMAL thinking on Flash-Lite & Flash Low for instant replies.
  • Fixed Slow Work Mode on Flash-Lite & Flash: Fast models now use direct non-streaming tool calls, skip heavy 2.4s OCR scans on non-GUI and fast steps, and trim pre/post-step dead waits.
  • Fixed Quota Burn & OpenRouter Validation: Eliminated 16× nested retry burn and duplicate parallel calls on the same model, fixed OpenRouter rejecting pasted keys as model IDs, and recognized Anthropic zero-credit keys so Claude UI still activates cleanly.
